Policies

The short version: we replace what arrives damaged, you own your measurements, and we ship in under a month.

Shipping and lead time

Every cabinet is built to order in our shop and ships flat-pack, nationwide, in under a month from the day you order. Shipping is calculated and charged at checkout.

Damaged in shipping

If a piece arrives damaged, we replace the piece.

Measurements

We build each cabinet to the exact width, height and depth you enter. If a measurement was wrong, you pay to have the piece remade at the corrected size. Before checkout you'll be asked to confirm your measurements.

Tips before you order:

  • Measure each opening at the top, middle and bottom. Use the smallest number.
  • Enter sizes in inches. Fractions like 34 1/2 work.
  • Check appliance specs for required openings.
  • Not sure? Send us your drawings and a rep will check them.
